BrightCell Budget Planner: Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: we collect no data.

  • Everything you type into BrightCell Budget Planner is stored only on your device. There is no account, no server and no cloud sync.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • The app works fully offline. It only connects to Google Play, through Google Play Billing, when you open the Premium screen, buy the optional one-time Premium unlock or tap Restore purchase.
  • In-app purchase: the Premium unlock is sold and processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your name, email, card or other payment details. The app only stores a local "Premium unlocked" flag on your device so it keeps working offline. Google's handling of the purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Backup and CSV/PDF files are only created when you tap Export, and they go wherever you choose (for example email, Files or Google Drive). We never see them.
  • Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system.
  • Deleting the app deletes its data. Keep a backup file if you might want it later.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. Google Play data safety: No data collected, no data shared.

Questions: contact BrightCellPlanStudio on Etsy.
