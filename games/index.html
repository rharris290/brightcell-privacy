BrightCell Games (Sandfall Sort, Big Card Patience, Word Garden) – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

BrightCell Games (Sandfall Sort, Big Card Patience, Word Garden) ("the game") is made by BrightCell, a solo independent developer. This policy explains what data the game handles.

Offline play

The game collects no personal data. Your progress, settings, streaks and statistics are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The game contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Online play (when enabled)

Online play is not yet available ("Online coming soon"). When it is enabled, the game server will store an anonymous player ID (a random identifier, not linked to your real identity) and the display name you choose, together with match results and daily scores needed for matchmaking and leaderboards. Your display name and scores may be visible to other players on leaderboards. There is no chat. We do not sell or share this data, and it is used only to run online play. You can ask us to delete your online data by emailing us with your display name.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

The Android app requests Internet access only so the optional online mode can connect to the game server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
